Auto Lyrics Sync

No song picked yet.
2 · Lyrics
Hindi, English, Urdu or mixed — Devanagari, Roman and Arabic script all work. Urdu lines render right-to-left.
3 · Sync — real audio alignment
Onset alignment listens to the actual vocal onsets in your song and snaps each word to the nearest detected onset — not equal spacing. Studio-grade word timing (WhisperX-style) needs a cloud model; use Manual Sync to fine-tune any word.
